Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · App: %1 Daha İyi (iOS)

In short: We collect no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent to us or to third parties.

Data stored

Your habits, to-dos, weekly schedule, focus sessions and settings are saved in a file inside the app's container on your device. There is no server, no account and no cloud sync.

App blocking (Screen Time / Family Controls)

If you enable app blocking during focus, the app uses Apple's Screen Time APIs. Apps you pick are given to us only as opaque tokens; we cannot see which apps they are. Tokens stay on your device. In social media mode the app uses a built-in list of well-known social media bundle identifiers; nothing about the apps you use is collected. You can revoke access anytime in Settings > Screen Time.

Notifications

Focus and daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Analytics, ads, third parties

The app contains no ads, tracking, analytics or third-party SDKs. Apple may share aggregated, anonymous app statistics with developers if you allow it in your device settings; this is governed by Apple's policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. We keep no copy.

See the support page.
